### RULES FOR USING THESE HINTS:
1. Use the hints ONLY to choose wording, register (tone of voice) and interjections for the listed lines. The source text remains the only source of meaning.
2. Do NOT add emotions that are not present in the source text. If the source words are emotionally flat, keep the translation flat.
3. Do NOT output the table, the emotion labels, the intensity values, the line numbers or any comment about them.
4. Do NOT change the number of lines or their order. A hint never justifies merging, splitting, dropping or reordering lines.
